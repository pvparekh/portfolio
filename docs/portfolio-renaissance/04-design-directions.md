# 04 — Art-direction exploration and selected approach

Research date: 2026-10-09. Source baseline: pvparekh/portfolio@01e6f9510952776fcf1a39266270b686a5c224a4 (feat/solutions-client-acquisition). This research branch is independent of production main. Method: GitHub/Vercel API inspection plus public websites through text/structure browsing; live rendered portfolio and animation/device interactions were NOT visually inspected.

## A. Systems Atlas — EDITORIAL, selected as foundation
- First viewport: asymmetric strong personal typographic statement; precise small-caption role/current-company line; primary work and contact actions.
- Career: grouped employer columns; role progression drawn as restrained nested chronology; all existing bullets rendered completely.
- Projects: one flagship Formula Vision technical story with authentic screen media and selectable architecture proof; two smaller dissimilar story forms for Review Bot and AetherFlow.
- Rhythm: charcoal surfaces, subtle amber indices, rule lines, alternating dense/easy spreads, no identical card grid by default.
- Strength: most legible in 15-second recruiter scan and 5-minute engineer review; low risk of turning into a SaaS template.
- Risk: over-editorializing may produce a text-heavy site. Countermeasure: real product media and concise but genuine architecture examples.

## B. Engineering Observatory — INTERACTIVE
- A restrained instrument metaphor: project panes feel like read-only windows into deployed systems, with actual telemetry and architecture data where verified.
- Focused interface transitions, optional interaction for Formula Vision and source-backed software demonstrations.
- Strength: differentiated sense of technical depth. Risk: performance, maintenance, misleading pseudo-data, touch and reduced-motion complexity.
- Verdict: adopt a *small, opt-in* subset only where real data exists; not the entire page grammar.

## C. Engineering Monograph — CASEBOOK
- Long-form case-study architecture: compact index of projects, expandable challenge → constraints → design decisions → tradeoffs → validation pages.
- Strength: excellent for senior-engineer assessment and future /projects. Risk: too much reading and difficult recruiter scanning at homepage level.
- Verdict: use as future /projects structural target; provide short previews and links on homepage.

## Chosen synthesis
**Systems Atlas backbone + one authentic Observatory moment + Monograph depth links.** Preserve existing palette, navigation identities and copy. Do not fabricate diagrams or fake live metrics. Start with token/interaction coherence, then employer proof and projects, then measured motion. This is a directional specification pending actual rendered-site evaluation.

## Design criteria
15-second identity clarity; every achievement accessible without animation; all projects distinguishable by purpose; brand is recognizable; no unsupported claims; full experience verbatim; six breakpoints; keyboard/dragless touch; Web Vitals measured. Favor A unless real screenshots show it does not translate to existing visual DNA.
