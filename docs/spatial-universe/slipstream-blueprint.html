The six speed lines become a world you travel through.

This is the creative and engineering plan for evolving the portfolio into a scroll-driven spatial experience. The amber streaks that cross today's hero become thousands of filaments at different depths. Scrolling moves a camera forward through them, and each section is a place where the stream slows and organizes around real content. All text stays in the page as normal HTML; the 3D layer is decoration that loads after the content and steps down gracefully on weaker devices.

The sketch above is a 2D canvas approximation of the look. The production version uses one WebGL instanced shader. Full documents live in the repository at docs/spatial-universe/ on branch docs/spatial-universe-blueprint.

Creative directions

Three directions, one recommendation

Weighted on continuity with the current identity, readability, solo maintainability and mobile viability.

RECOMMENDED77

Slipstream

The streaks become a luminous slipstream in depth. Sections are sectors on a lap: grid, halo, straight, three systems, overview, horizon. One bespoke shader, no 3D assets.

ARCHITECTURAL40

Monolith Atlas

Dark monumental rooms cut by amber light. Premium stills, but heavy baked assets, weak long-text integration, and the streaks become secondary.

DATA LANDSCAPE63

Signal Terrain

A terrain of light samples with signals flowing along contours. Original, but close to the synthwave trope, and the name loses its centre. Its flow vocabulary is reused for Data Solutions.

Homepage storyboard

Six sectors, one continuous take

The camera holds still while text is readable and moves between sections. Its position depends only on scroll position, so scrolling back up replays the journey exactly in reverse.

Solutions pages

Same universe, different physics

Each page keeps the shared material but behaves according to what it says. Neither reuses the homepage lap.

Delivery

Eleven milestones, none of them on main

  3. M2Filament shader and hero transformation, with the name-opens transit

  6. M5Data Solutions: Confluence
